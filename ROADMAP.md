# ROADMAP.md: Product Evolution

## Phase 1: Alpha Prototype (Current)
- Complete two-sided AI communication mediator architecture.
- Person A private intake with Reconcile.
- Neutral translation and invitation generation.
- Person B private intake with Intention vs. Impact resolution.
- Joint Mediation Bridge (The Aha Moment, Common Ground, Starter Messages).
- Flagship Parent ↔ Child Demo.
- Server-side role isolation and strict confidentiality.

## Phase 2: Beta Launch
- Live web-socket multi-party joint conversation (Reconcile intervening live when tension spikes).
- Email & SMS invitation links with one-time verification tokens.
- User accounts and encrypted conversation history.
- Emotional temperature gauge during live conversations.

## Phase 3: WhatsApp Communication Bridge
- WhatsApp Business API integration.
- Vent to Reconcile over WhatsApp.
- Reconcile contacts the recipient on WhatsApp with neutral framing.
- Three-way asynchronous mediation thread.

## Phase 4: Expansion
- Multi-party family & group mediation (e.g., both parents + multiple siblings).
- Workplace & co-founder conflict resolution mode.
- Voice-first mediation companion with audio pacing.
