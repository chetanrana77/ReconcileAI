# PRODUCT.md: Product Specification

## 1. Core Concept & Product Promise
**Reconcile AI** is an AI Communication Mediator.
When two people cannot talk without conflict, one person talks to Reconcile first.
Reconcile understands what they are actually trying to communicate — their emotions, intent, concerns, and underlying needs.
Instead of forwarding angry or reactive words, Reconcile communicates the underlying issue to the other person in a neutral, compassionate way.
Reconcile then talks privately to the second person, understands their side, and helps both people communicate constructively.

### Core Promises:
- *"Reconcile is the AI you talk to when you don't know how to talk to them."*
- *"Your words stay private. We help the other person understand what you mean."*
- *"Don't pick a side. Understand both."*
- *"Intention is not the same as impact."*

## 2. Emotional Stance & Brand Personality
- **A Calm, Emotionally Intelligent Common Friend**: Not ChatGPT, not a clinical therapist, not a judge, not a corporate HR chatbot.
- **Validating Emotions, Challenging Assumptions**: Emotions are always validated (*"I hear why that felt hurtful"*); assumptions about the other person's intent are gently explored (*"Feeling ignored and being intentionally ignored aren't necessarily the same thing"*).
- **The Translator**: Translates emotionally charged language into underlying relational needs (*"You never listen to me"* &rarr; *"I need to feel heard and valued by you"*).

## 3. The 3-Way Journey
```
PERSON A (Private Consultation)
       ↕
RECONCILE AI (Neutral Translator & Mediator)
       ↕
PERSON B (Private Consultation)
       ↓
JOINT MEDIATION BRIDGE (Shared Clarity)
```

1. **Person A Intake**: Conversational dialogue with adaptive follow-ups.
2. **Intent & Need Extraction**: Separating reactive anger from core needs.
3. **Invitation Creation**: A neutral translation preview that Person A reviews and shares.
4. **Person B Intake**: Person B joins with their own confidential chat without seeing Person A's raw words.
5. **Intention vs. Impact**: Exploring the disconnect between what was intended and what was felt.
6. **Mediation Bridge**:
   - Both sides heard and validated.
   - Disconnect analysis (Intention vs. Impact).
   - Common ground.
   - Personalized starter messages each can send to open direct dialogue.
   - Concrete next step agreement.

## 4. Flagship Event Demo: Parent ↔ Child
- **Child**: "My parents keep asking about my studies. I know they care, but I feel like they don't trust me."
- **Parent**: "I'm terrified they'll fall behind. I only ask because I love them."
- **The Gap**: Anxious parental love colliding with emerging adolescent autonomy.
- **The Resolution**: Scheduled weekly check-in, pausing daily interrogation.
