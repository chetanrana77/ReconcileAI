# DEMO_MODE.md: Pre-built Mediation Scenarios

## 1. Flagship Scenario: Parent ↔ Child (`demo-parent-child`)
- **Child's Initial Share**:
  *"My parents keep asking about my studies. I know they're worried, but it feels like they don't trust me."*
- **Reconcile Reflection**:
  *"It sounds like the questions make you feel like they don't trust you to manage your own responsibilities. Is that close to what you're feeling?"*
- **Child's Response**:
  *"Yes, exactly. I want to tell them on my own terms without feeling hovered over."*
- **Reconcile Proposal**:
  *"If you want, I can help your parents understand what you're actually trying to say — without sending your raw words."*
- **Neutral Invitation to Parent**:
  *"Your child asked me to help explain something that's been difficult to say directly. They feel overwhelmed by frequent check-ins and want more independence, while still knowing that you care and believe in them."*
- **Parent's Private Share**:
  *"I ask because college admissions are tough and I see them on their phone all the time. I'm terrified they'll fall behind. I only ask because I love them."*
- **Reconcile Insight (Intention vs. Impact)**:
  *"Your intention is guidance and protective love. But the impact on them was feeling doubted. Both can be true at the same time."*
- **The Mediation Bridge**:
  - *Person A Side*: Striving for adult autonomy, needing trust.
  - *Person B Side*: Anxious protective love, fear of regret.
  - *Disconnect*: Intent was care; impact was micromanagement.
  - *Common Ground*: Both want the child to succeed; both hate daily tension.
  - *Agreement*: Scheduled 15-minute weekly Sunday sync, pausing daily check-ins.
