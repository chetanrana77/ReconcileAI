# DESIGN_SYSTEM.md: UI & Design Guidelines

## 1. Visual Tone & Aesthetic
- **Calm, Premium Consumer Companion**: Linear + Headspace aesthetic.
- **Emotionally Intelligent Simplicity**: Focuses on clarity, generous padding, and reduced cognitive load during emotional tension.
- **No Chatbot Clutter**: Uses conversational cards and contextual suggestions instead of endless generic messaging bubbles.

## 2. Color Palette
- **Primary**: Deep Indigo/Violet (`indigo-600` / `#4F46E5`) — grounded, thoughtful.
- **Backgrounds**: Warm Off-White (`#FAF9F7`) — prevents cold, clinical white glare.
- **Role Accents**:
  - Person A (Left/Initial): Indigo (`#6366F1`)
  - Person B (Right/Counterpart): Violet (`#8B5CF6`)
  - Shared Confluence / Bridge: Emerald (`#10B981`)
- **Text**: Dark Slate (`#1A1A2E` headings, `#4A4A6A` body).

## 3. Typography & Spacing
- **Primary Font**: `Inter` (sans-serif)
- **Line Heights**: Generous (1.6) for readability.
- **Micro-Interactions**: Subtle fade-ins (`fade-in-up`), soft borders, non-jarring feedback.
